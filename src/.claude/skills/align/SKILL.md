name: align
description: before planning, investigating and making any decision or changes; there has to be genuine shared understanding, rather than assumptions or guesses.
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of an idea until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the decision tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one. For each question, provide your recommended answer.

Ask the questions one at a time, waiting for feedback on each question before continuing. Asking multiple questions at once is bewildering.

If a _fact_ can be found by exploring the environment (filesystem, tools, etc.), look it up rather than asking me. The _decisions_, though, are mine — put each one to me and wait for my answer.

alignment.md: Build a short list of facts and the agreed decisions so both sides are looking at the same thing.
`alignment.md` should always be created at the root of the opened project. If there is already an existing `alignment.md`, say so and stop.
Do not act on it.
Keep aligning until I confirm we have reached a shared understanding.
