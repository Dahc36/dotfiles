# The choice that is cheap to undo wins

A decision made without me is a guess at what I would have chosen.
Some guesses are wrong, and what a wrong one costs is the work of undoing it.

## What is built on a choice is the cost of undoing it

A choice is cheap to undo while little depends on it.
Whatever others come to rely on grows more expensive with each thing built on top.
Between options that serve the goal, the one that leaves the least to take apart wins.

## A better guess does not outweigh a cheaper mistake

An option that looks better but is expensive to undo bets everything built after it on the guess being right.
The cheap option risks only itself.
The expensive one is justified only when no cheap option serves the goal.
