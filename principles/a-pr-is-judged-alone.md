# A PR is cut where a reviewer can judge it alone

A PR is the unit a reviewer says yes or no to.
They can only say it about a change whose purpose and correctness show within it.

## One purpose

A reviewer judges a change against what it is for.
A PR with two purposes is judged twice, and neither half can be approved without the other.

## Whole enough to judge

A change whose reason only appears in a later PR asks the reviewer to trust what they have not seen.
What a PR introduces, it also uses or proves, so that its worth shows without leaving it.

## Small enough to hold

Past what a reviewer can hold, they skim.
Between two cuts that each stand alone, the smaller PRs win.
